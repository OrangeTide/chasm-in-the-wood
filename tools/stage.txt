# Pulls the exact members the manifest needs out of the read-only kit archives into the
# staging dir, so nothing writes back to the archives. Members carry spaces and brackets,
# so the three fields are separated by a vertical bar, not whitespace:
#
#   <archive under ASSET_ARCHIVES> | <member path inside the archive> | <dest under STAGE_DIR>
#
# The dest is the path the manifest (tools/assets.txt) then reads under ASSET_ROOT.
